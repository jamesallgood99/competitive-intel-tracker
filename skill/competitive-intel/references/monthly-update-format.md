# Monthly update format

Published on the first Tuesday of each month. Covers everything since the previous update.

## 1. TL;DR

The three things a rep most needs to know before their next call, across everything below. One sentence
each, linked to the battlecard when it's about one competitor.

## 2. The market

Four short sections, each with a one- or two-sentence summary and two to four items with sources:

- **Industry trends:** what changed in the market that a rep should know.
- **Regulatory updates:** rules proposed, passed or enforced.
- **Mergers and acquisitions:** deals, funding rounds, shutdowns.
- **Industry health:** pricing, profitability and demand signals.

If nothing material happened in a section, say so in one line.

## 3. Competitor movers

For each competitive category, the top three movers (fewer if fewer moved). For each:

- **What changed:** one or two sentences.
- **What to do:** one or two sentences on what reps should say or do differently.

Then one line listing the competitors that were tracked but quiet.

### Picking movers

Score each change: severity (major 3, minor 1) times a type weight (product and pricing 1.5, compliance
and news 1.25, messaging and field intel 1, customer reviews 0.75, employee reviews 0.5). Sum per
competitor over the window. Rank within each category; break ties by number of changes.
