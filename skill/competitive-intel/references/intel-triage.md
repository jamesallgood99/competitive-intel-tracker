# Intel triage

For each message a rep posts in the intel channel:

1. **Is it intel?** It needs a concrete claim about a competitor: a product, a price, a deal, a message, a
   hire, a partnership. Questions to the team, small talk and opinions without a fact are not intel.
2. **Which competitor?** Match to a tracked competitor. If it's about a company we don't track, suggest
   the rep request a battlecard instead.
3. **Rewrite it for the card.** One or two neutral sentences that keep every concrete fact (numbers,
   product names, timing) and add nothing. Keep customer names only if the rep wrote them; the admin
   decides whether they stay.
4. **Classify it.** Type: product, pricing, messaging, deal, people, partnership, other. Severity: major
   if it changes how we sell (a new product, a price change, a lost deal, a partnership), else minor.
5. **Flag missing sources.** If the rep didn't say where it came from, flag it for the admin.

The admin approves, edits or rejects. Rejections go to the rep privately.

## Examples

> "lol watchpost again"

Not intel.

> "Watchpost offered Acme Foods a free 90-day pilot to stop our eval, their IT guy hated the cloud blurring"

Intel on Watchpost AI, type deal, severity major. Card text: "Watchpost offered a free 90-day pilot at a
food processor to block our evaluation. The prospect's IT lead was uneasy about cloud-side blurring."
